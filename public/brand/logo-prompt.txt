Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square high-resolution raster graphic logo for LIU'S GATE, a University of Glasgow urban analytics research laboratory. This is a symbol only, with NO TEXT.
Primary request: Design one distinctive, compact, beautifully balanced isometric sandstone triumphal gateway. It should feel like a refined miniature architectural voxel model, aligned with a thoughtful academic website that uses an isometric Glasgow-inspired miniature city.
Subject: A single recognizable Scottish sandstone ceremonial gate or gatehouse with a strongly readable central open arch, two substantial piers, restrained block masonry, a carefully stepped cornice and a few crisply modeled volumes. A small, restrained cluster of adjacent voxel blocks subtly suggests an urban street grid and spatial data. The arch opening must remain visibly open.
Style/medium: precise polished isometric pixel / micro-voxel architecture, 30-degree isometric view, hard flat edges, modest architectural detail, sophisticated editorial identity. Strong silhouette that remains legible at 48 pixels. Minimal objects and clean geometry; avoid noise and fuzzy coarse pixels.
Composition/framing: one centered isolated symbol, approximately 88% of a square canvas, small consistent safety margin, no clipping. Compact mark rather than a large city illustration.

Lighting/mood: restrained soft directional illumination expressed by clear planar shading, professional academic confidence and a little architectural charm.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with an alpha channel. All empty space, including the gateway opening, must be transparent. No background color, no ground plane extending beyond the compact mark, no environmental scene.
Constraints: absolutely no letters, words, numerals, Chinese characters, inscriptions, slogans, mascots, people, vehicles, banners, decorative frames, badges, or watermarks. No fake checkerboard pattern. No white square or solid backdrop. No blur, glow, lens effects, photographic textures, excessively rounded clay forms, or gradient wallpaper. Deliver one high-resolution square logo.
